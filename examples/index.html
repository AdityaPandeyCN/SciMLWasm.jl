SciMLWasm

SciML solvers compiled to WebAssembly. Everything below runs in your browser with no Julia server.

  • Type your own equations

    Any first-order system up to 8 states, parsed to bytecode and solved by either integrator. Nothing recompiles when you change the equations.

  • Robertson, Rosenbrock23

    The standard stiff test problem: rate constants spanning nine orders of magnitude, integrated over five decades of time.

  • Van der Pol, adaptive Tsit5

    A limit-cycle oscillator with error control and dense output. The step size collapses at each relaxation jump.

  • Lorenz, fixed-step Tsit5

    The chaotic attractor, re-solved on every slider move.
